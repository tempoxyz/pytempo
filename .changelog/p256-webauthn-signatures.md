---
pytempo: minor
---

Added P256 and WebAuthn signing and verification that match viem (and tempo-primitives, down to how WebAuthn clientDataJSON is parsed)'s tempo Accounts byte for byte, in the new `pytempo.signatures` module: `P256Account` and `WebAuthnAccount` (headless, like `Account.fromP256` and `Account.fromHeadlessWebAuthn`), `Secp256k1Account`, `P256Signature`, `WebAuthnSignature`, and `recover_signer`, which verifies any Tempo signature envelope (secp256k1, P256, WebAuthn, and Keychain V1 or V2 with any inner signature) as tempo-primitives does. Added `TempoTransaction.sign_with`, `TempoTransaction.sign_access_key_with` and `TempoTransaction.recover_signer`, `KeychainSignature.sign_with` for P256 and WebAuthn access keys, and `KeyAuthorization.sign_with` for any root key. `KeychainSignature` now accepts any inner signature, and `SignedKeyAuthorization.decode` and `TempoTransaction.decode` accept and recover P256 and WebAuthn signatures (sender, keychain access key and key authorization) instead of raising `NotImplementedError`. Declared `pycryptodome` as a direct dependency.
